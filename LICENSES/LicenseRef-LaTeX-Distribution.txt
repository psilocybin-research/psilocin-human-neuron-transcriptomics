The unmodified Siglum / BusyTeX / TeX Live compiler files under explorer/vendor/latex retain their individual upstream licences. These include MIT for Siglum and BusyTeX, GPL for linked TeX Live engine components, LPPL for LaTeX packages, and the GUST Font License for Latin Modern. Full component notices and original font terms are in LICENSE-COMPONENTS.txt; MIT and GPL texts are retained separately in that directory. UPSTREAM.json identifies the pinned unmodified build. SOURCE-ACCESS.json provides the public corresponding-source location and original source hashes. Source archives are available at https://psilocybin-research.com/assets/account-latex/source/ with filenames and SHA-256 recorded in SOURCE-ACCESS.json. The compiler runtime package does not include those large development source archives.
