# Analysis status and chronology

This repository distinguishes prespecified, post-primary and post hoc work.

## Primary analysis

The metabolic and redox pathway plan was frozen locally on 18 September 2026 before the new pathway outcomes were inspected. It was not externally registered. The source article, upstream code, published differential-expression findings and technical QC were already known. The exact plan and cryptographic freeze record are preserved in `reports/analysis_plan_frozen.md` and `provenance/analysis_freeze.json`.

## Targeted redox tier

The NRF2 target panel, glutathione module, pentose-phosphate pathway and GPX4 sentinel were specified after the primary and original secondary pathway results had been inspected. They form a separate eight-test family and are not presented as prospectively confirmatory evidence.

## OXPHOS symbol-alias sensitivity

The HGNC alias analysis was performed after the primary OXPHOS result was known. It tests whether historical ATP5 symbols changed the conclusion and does not replace the frozen literal-symbol estimates or their 15-test multiplicity correction.

## DNA-maintenance follow-up

DNA repair and telomere maintenance were included in the original frozen six-test exploratory family. Their decomposition, overlap-removal checks, cell-cycle controls and cross-day concordance were specified after those exploratory results were known. The 24 follow-up tests use a separate correction family and remain explicitly post hoc and hypothesis-generating.

No additional same-dataset pathway search was added after this bounded follow-up. Functional mitochondrial activity, DNA-repair activity, telomere length and telomere protection were not measured.
