# OXPHOS gene–pathway membership view

This descriptive display uses the frozen 113-gene OXPHOS leading-edge union, archived gene-level DESeq2 outputs and literal frozen gene-set memberships. It does not refit models or introduce additional significance tests. The 202 bipartite edges connect each included gene to every archived gene set containing that symbol. Pathway diamonds represent those actual sets. No gene–gene interaction or inferred biological-activity edge is constructed.

## Identity and structural annotations

The build requires exactly 113 unique archived symbols and one selected rank representative per symbol. Versioned Ensembl identifiers must agree with `config/oxphos_complex_membership.json`; unresolved identities fail the build. The mapping records matched UniProt accessions, names, assignment basis and source links. It uses the locally archived public-context snapshot retrieved on 17 November 2026, reviewed on 18 November 2026. These external annotations are distinct from experimental measurements and do not establish identity across annotation releases.

Structural groups contain 20 complex I, 3 complex II, 8 complex III and 10 complex IV genes. The remaining 72 genes are retained separately. No complex V structural subunit occurs in this selected union. The seven V-type ATPase genes are not mitochondrial complex V. COX10 (heme synthesis), COX17 (copper delivery/assembly) and COX7A2L/SCAF1 (supercomplex assembly) are not counted as structural complex IV subunits. NDUFAB1 is retained as a complex I accessory subunit. Assignment references and exception sources accompany the mapping. The absence of complex V from a selected leading-edge union does not establish absence of a complex V transcriptional response.

## Encoding and interaction

All gene spheres have equal size. A single symmetric blue–white–rust scale covers every finite log₂ fold change across both day/common-control contrasts. Its positive endpoint is the maximum absolute value rounded upward to the next half unit, with a minimum of one; no finite estimate is clipped. Grey denotes an unavailable estimate. Pathway diamonds have a separate categorical appearance. A gold outline identifies the selected gene, not significance.

Coordinates are deterministic display assignments grouped by curated subunit class, with pathway nodes on a separate plane. They are not molecular coordinates, interaction strengths, inferred clusters or effect magnitudes. Force-graph rendering is used with fixed coordinates and no browser force-layout simulation. Group labels are annotations rather than invented nodes. Complex filters retain all original genes in the underlying download, and complex V explicitly shows an empty selection.

Selection exposes the stored gene identifier and exact numeric precision for all three archived contrasts: log₂ fold change, standard error, Wald statistic, nominal p-value and gene-level BH FDR. Search and a keyboard-accessible gene list provide an alternative to canvas selection. Downloads include the complete node/edge snapshot, assignment provenance and all archived gene statistics. A copied snapshot is passed to the renderer so its object mutations cannot alter archived inputs.

Both day contrasts use the available common control pool. They do not identify a treatment-by-time interaction. Gene FDR and pathway-family FDR remain separate. A selected leading-edge union is not a transcriptome-wide random sample; node density, distance and prominence support no statistical inference. This display does not establish respiration, ATP synthesis, protein abundance or molecular interactions.

Synchronized comparison displays Day 1 and Day 3 simultaneously with the same frozen node coordinates, common colour domain and linked orbit-camera target/position in both directions. Selection and filters apply to both. Views are side by side on desktop and stacked on mobile. The second renderer is created only on request and destroyed on exit; devices unable to create it retain single-view switching and all exact values. Both views retain the common-control qualification: visual differences do not establish a treatment-by-time interaction.

## Implementation and validation

The static overview WebP below the main actions is captured from this actual network and does not execute 3D code. Clicking it opens browser fullscreen, with a Framework7 sheet as fallback and an immediate Framework7 preloader. No additional route or page is created. The app retains its selected appearance; the canvas has a fixed dark substrate. Automatic low-strength bloom applies to bright categorical pathway markers and is decorative, not an encoding of confidence, effect or activity. It is off by default on mobile. Floating controls use decorative glass blending while text and the scientific colour scale are unblended. Search, filters and provenance open in native Framework7 push sheets. Selected-gene evidence uses a side panel on wide displays and a push sheet on smaller displays; the canvas extends behind all controls. The pinned 3d-force-graph and Three.js renderer is bundled locally, loaded on demand and included in the core offline inventory. Original dependency licence notices are retained in `third_party_licenses/network-3d.txt`. Existing statistical plots, navigation and heatmap groups are preserved. Rendering pauses when the viewer is off screen or the document is hidden. Geometry, materials, textures and observers are disposed when the view is removed. WebGL failure leaves search, exact values and downloads usable.

Data tests verify the 113 identities, structural group counts and exceptions, exact archived statistics, every membership edge and the common colour range. Browser checks exercise real WebGL at mobile and desktop widths, delayed renderer loading, time-point switching, selection, filters, camera controls, fullscreen, exports and sheet reopening. Offline and fallback checks accompany release validation.

## Linked research context

Gene selection coordinates the membership focus, three archived gene contrasts and nine VST profiles. Wald intervals are calculated as log₂FC ± 1.96 SE; unavailable estimates are omitted from interval marks and labelled in the table. Summary values are rounded, with original precision retained in numeric-cell tooltips and the full-statistics disclosure. Profile symbols and colours identify differentiation blocks. The nine pooled block-by-condition profiles are not nine independent replicates, and no individual-cell trajectory is drawn.

The separate, lazily loaded research snapshot copies the archived OXPHOS ranked-enrichment sensitivities, both CAMERA correlation settings and all ten Bayesian prior/likelihood settings. CAMERA retains its 15-test family FDR rather than substituting the within-contrast correction. The Bayesian model concerns paired block-level scores from 182 mapped genes, not the selected 113-gene network or gene-level posterior confidence. Estimated-correlation CAMERA results and prior-sensitive intervals remain visible. Source hashes accompany the sensitivity download.

Protein records come from the hosted UniProt snapshot matched by Ensembl gene ID. Opening coordinates is explicit and uses a prepared hosted model; the large molecular library is not added to the core PWA package. Saved annotations and previews remain available when unsaved coordinates cannot be opened offline. Protein selection resets the structure and feature context. Cartoon and surface representations do not imply measured molecular changes.

Residue highlighting requires an exact, unique, contiguous match of observed Cα residues against the selected canonical protein sequence (at least 20 residues unless the entire sequence is matched). Author chain IDs, residue numbers and insertion codes are preserved. Unknown/modified residues, ambiguous repeats, gaps and unmatched sequences are not assigned by guessed offsets or cross-reference coverage strings. Only mapped feature positions are highlighted, with coverage reported. This deliberately conservative mapping may leave valid but incomplete or engineered structures unhighlightable. Deposited method/resolution, predicted local pLDDT, snapshot retrieval and attribution retain their separate meanings. Separate protein models never form an inferred physical assembly.

The presentation layer includes a short shader-driven grid/light transition behind the fixed network when Framework7 dialogs or evidence panels open or close. It is a UI-state cue, not molecular dynamics or activity. Hover animates the outline only; gene spheres retain equal size and original effect colours. Bloom remains restricted to decorative high-intensity categorical accents. Reduced-motion preferences disable temporal effects, and transitions stop after settling or when the document is hidden. The overview preview retains fixed geometry during pointer/touch feedback.

Cross-day comparison links an accessible two-dimensional scatterplot to the same selected gene in both network views and the interval panel. Both axes retain the common symmetric effect limits. The equality diagonal is descriptive, not a test of the between-day difference. Statistical filters retain all genes, require estimable effects and nonnegative standard errors on both days, or apply archived gene-level BH FDR thresholds (0.05/0.01 on either day; 0.05 on both days). Both-day and either-day criteria are explicit; no new correction is computed within the selected union, and either-day selection does not constitute a joint hypothesis test. Counts combine the complex and statistical filters and apply identically to both 3D views and the scatterplot. Missing coordinates are omitted from scatter marks and counted separately. An empty result remains empty rather than relaxing a threshold.
