# Correlation-aware pathway sensitivity methods

This post hoc analysis was specified after the primary and Bayesian results were known. All five original Hallmark sets and three contrasts were retained. The nine block–condition count profiles used the original count filter and 21,098 expression-selected symbol representatives. DESeq2 median-ratio size factors were reconstructed from all 21,154 filtered genes without differential-expression refitting. Effective library sizes equal the size factor divided by its geometric mean, multiplied by the geometric mean of the filtered library sums. limma 3.68.5 voom used these effective sizes, no additional normalization and span 0.5; observation precision weights entered expression-based CAMERA under ~ block + condition. The model had five coefficients and four residual degrees of freedom.

The principal sensitivity estimated each set's gene correlation (inter.gene.cor=NA); the declared comparison used the documented default fixed value of 0.01. Both used directional parametric CAMERA statistics, use.ranks=FALSE, allow.neg.cor=FALSE and trend.var=FALSE. Correlations were estimated from standardized weighted residual coordinates after fitting all block and condition terms, not from raw expression correlations across conditions. The limma 3.68.5 implementation also changes the parametric test reference degrees of freedom: min(residual df, G-2)=4 for estimated correlation versus G-2=21,096 for fixed correlation (G=21,098 genes). Thus, the estimated-versus-fixed comparison changes both variance inflation and the reference distribution. Negative correlations could not deflate variance. BH adjustment covered all 15 tests separately within each setting, rather than only the five sets within each contrast. No setting was selected after seeing results.

The files in `tables/camera_sensitivity/` provide every test, complete mapping and sample normalization. This repository supplies executable code, frozen specifications, derived pooled counts, software source checksums and numerical outputs. The original primary RDS is not required. OXPHOS mapped 182 genes. Estimated-correlation CAMERA retained upward direction at both days but neither was significant (Day 1 p=0.274, q=0.587; Day 3 p=0.235, q=0.587). Corresponding correlations were 0.318 and 0.266, and variance-inflation factors 58.6 and 49.2. None of the 15 tests passed q<0.05 under estimated correlation. Fixed correlation 0.01 yielded upward OXPHOS at both days (both q=5.84e-8), with a negative treated-day contrast (q=0.00467).

All outcomes must be read together. This test accounts for gene correlation conditional on its estimate or assumed value; four residual degrees of freedom limit estimation. It remains a competitive test of the same biological samples, not independent replication. Differences from DESeq2/fgsea also involve expression and precision modeling. Unknown control harvest-time composition limits treatment attribution in either day/common-control comparison. Statistical significance of the treated-day contrast under fixed correlation does not supply a treatment-by-time interaction or supersede the original directional classification.
