# Exploratory mitochondrial follow-up

MitoCarta3.0 annotations are external biological context. They extend the gene cards and complete respiratory-complex browser beyond the selected 113-gene leading-edge union. Their integration does not modify the original estimates, primary enrichment, CAMERA analyses or manuscript. Curated membership may include alternative subunits across biological contexts and does not describe one simultaneous assembly.

The human workbook and 149-pathway GMX are pinned by SHA-256. The source's 2020-01-30 Ensembl mappings are preserved, including one-to-many mappings. Joining uses exact stable Ensembl identifiers without symbol rescue. Of 1,136 records, 1,074 match one archived gene record; 62 have no archived result. No reason for absence is inferred. Source UniProt accessions do not identify the expressed protein isoform. All mappings are available in the downloadable audit.

The enrichment is a post-primary investigation motivated by the OXPHOS finding. Before computing its new results, the gene sets, ranked universe, mapping rules, size thresholds, contrasts, seed, numerical parameters and reporting family were recorded in [the frozen plan](../config/mitocarta_followup_v1.json). This limits additional analytical flexibility; it does not make the investigation independent or confirmatory.

`fgsea::fgseaMultilevel` 1.38.0 uses all 21,154 finite archived signed Wald statistics for each common-control contrast. There are no duplicate stable IDs or tied statistics. Only uniquely mapped MitoCarta records enter the pathway sets. Matched size 10–500 admits 76 pathways per contrast. One BH family includes all 152 tested pathway–contrast pairs; all 298 candidate rows, including exclusions, are exported. The fixed numerical floor is 1e-10; results at that floor are not resolved below it. Overlapping hierarchy parents and children are not independent discoveries. No new correlation-aware sensitivity test was added; the existing CAMERA sensitivity findings remain relevant.

At joint BH q < 0.05, 38 tested pathways meet the threshold at Day 1 and 40 at Day 3. These counts include overlapping sets. Complete results, coverage, leading edges and exclusions are in [all_pathways.csv](../tables/mitocarta_followup/all_pathways.csv), with [provenance](../tables/mitocarta_followup/provenance.json). A difference in significance between contrasts is not evidence of a difference between them. Three differentiation blocks and unresolved common-control harvest timing continue to constrain interpretation.

The paired enrichment plot uses one fixed symmetric NES axis for both days and all display scopes. The initial biological overview selects respiratory structural/assembly sets, translation, ribosome, import and TCA cycle by function rather than significance. All tested pathways are available in a second scope. Filled versus hollow dots encode joint BH q < 0.05 versus q ≥ 0.05; sets excluded by size have explicit labels and no assigned NES. No uncertainty interval, temporal difference or mitochondrial activity is inferred from dot separation. Selecting a result shows exact statistics, complete membership and the contrast-specific selected leading edge. The respiratory-chain diagram is an external organizational reference; it does not represent measured flux or physical distances.

The complex-I pilot preserves all 45 deposited protein chains in human PDB 9TI4 assembly 1. Its assembly operation is identity-only. PDBe SIFTS maps those chains to 44 distinct UniProt/gene records; the repeated chain copies receive the same gene estimate. Only deposited Cα positions are displayed. Traces connect spatially adjacent, consecutively numbered resolved residues; unresolved gaps are not reconstructed. Source mappings include sequence identity and coverage. A fixed ±2.5 log₂FC scale applies to both days; grey denotes missing/non-estimable mappings and gold marks selection. No protein change or respiratory-function measurement is implied.

Rebuild annotations explicitly with `python explorer/scripts/build-mitochondrial-context.py --source-dir SOURCE_DIRECTORY` (requires xlrd); source bytes are hash-checked. Download the coordinate and SIFTS URLs recorded in the pilot metadata to the same directory, then run `python explorer/scripts/build-complex-i-pilot.py --source-dir SOURCE_DIRECTORY`. Run `Rscript src/transcriptomics/mitocarta_followup.R` in an R library environment containing the pinned fgsea version, jsonlite and digest. App builds consume the checked-in snapshots and never run the analysis or contact annotation providers.
